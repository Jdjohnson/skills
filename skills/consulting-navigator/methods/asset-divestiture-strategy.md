# Asset Divestiture Strategy
## Purpose
Design and sequence the sale of non-core assets to raise capital, cut complexity, and fund the remaining core. It turns a plausible sale into a buyer-ready, separable transaction plan rather than merely labeling an asset non-core.
## Use
- Freeing capital or reducing complexity by selling non-core businesses, plants, or product lines.
- Restructuring a distressed or over-levered portfolio under time pressure.
- Responding to a credible inbound interest that forces a hold-vs-sell decision.
## Avoid
- You only need a valuation screen without a sell process design—use divestiture-analysis.
- The question is pure portfolio role diagnosis with no near-term sale—use portfolio-strategy-analysis or corporate-parenting-advantage.
- Cash is the immediate constraint and no saleable asset is ready—use liquidity-management first.
## Inputs
**Must-have:** Portfolio map of assets/units with ownership, EBITDA, and strategic fit; indicative valuation ranges and likely buyer universe; stranded-cost, TSA, and separation complexity estimates; capital needs and use-of-proceeds priorities; legal, tax, labor, and regulatory constraints on a sale.

**Nice-to-have:** carve-out financial statements, buyer indications, separation precedents, employee-transfer data, and a data-room index.

**When data is thin:** establish a decision range and an explicit diligence list; do not imply that an early valuation is an executable deal price.
## Procedure
1. **Set the divestiture thesis.** State why the asset should leave, what the parent retains, the required proceeds, and the deadline. Separate a strategic exit from a liquidity bridge.
2. **Draw the perimeter.** List legal entities, contracts, people, IP, inventory, systems, sites, and shared services that move, stay, or require a transition service agreement (TSA).
3. **Screen buyers and routes.** Compare strategic buyers, financial sponsors, management sale, and carve-out alternatives for price, certainty, antitrust, and execution burden.
4. **Model net value to the seller.** Start with gross proceeds, then subtract taxes, separation spend, TSA cost, lost shared-cost absorption, and required reinvestment in the retained core.
5. **Design separation and sequencing.** Put diligence, approvals, consultation, signing, closing, and TSA exit on one critical path; expose decisions that must precede buyer outreach.
6. **Authorize the sale plan.** Recommend a route, reserve price logic, negotiation guardrails, accountable owners, and contingency if the preferred buyer withdraws.
## Output Contract
A divestiture blueprint containing the strategic thesis, precisely defined perimeter, buyer universe, net-value range, separation workplan, TSA principles, risk register, governance, and use-of-proceeds decision. It must show what is being sold, to whom, by when, and what the parent must still fund after closing.
## Evidence
Treat entity ownership, contracts, headcount, and historic EBITDA as observed. Mark buyer appetite, multiple ranges, and standalone cost estimates as inferred or assumed. Reconcile the perimeter to legal and financial reporting; keep seller-only dis-synergies visible. If a key contract cannot transfer, flag it as a closing condition rather than silently assuming continuity.
## Checks
- The asset perimeter has no unowned shared service or IP dependency.
- Net proceeds include taxes, stranded costs, and one-time separation spending.
- At least two credible buyer paths have been tested for certainty as well as price.
- TSA duration has an exit owner and a service-level boundary.
- The retained business has a funded plan for costs it can no longer share.
## Failure Modes
- Selling the revenue stream while forgetting the people and systems that deliver it.
- Selecting the highest headline bid even though regulatory approval makes closing improbable.
- Treating stranded corporate costs as if they disappear on day one.
- Letting a temporary TSA become an unmanaged operating dependency.
- Spending proceeds before tax, debt, and separation obligations are quantified.
