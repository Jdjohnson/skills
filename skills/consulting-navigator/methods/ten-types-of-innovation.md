# Ten Types of Innovation
## Purpose
Analyze and expand innovation options across ten business-system types rather than product features alone. It broadens the option space across configuration, offering, and experience choices before a roadmap selects what to build.
## Use
- Auditing where a firm innovates across product, process, and business-system dimensions
- Generating non-feature innovation options (profit model, network, structure, experience)
- Comparing competitive innovation mixes before choosing a strategy stack
## Avoid
- You need to rank known features for one release—use feature-prioritization or kano-model
- The need is industry structure diagnosis—use five-forces or blue-ocean-strategy alone
- Execution sequencing is the issue—use product-roadmap-development or stage-gate-process
## Inputs
**Must-have:** Current offer, business model, and value-chain or ecosystem map; competitive examples of innovation beyond product features; strategic constraints (brand, capital, partners, regulation); priority customer and economic outcomes innovation must serve.
## Procedure
1. **Frame the innovation ambition.** Name the customer, economic, and strategic outcome to improve. Establish constraints that make an option infeasible, such as regulated pricing, channel commitments, or a protected brand promise.
2. **Map the current innovation mix.** Inventory how the business makes money, connects with partners, organizes work, runs processes, configures offers, supports customers, uses channels, builds brands, and shapes customer engagement. Mark where past investment is concentrated.
3. **Scan analogs by type.** Collect examples from direct competitors and adjacent industries, classifying the mechanism rather than copying the visible feature. Ask what changed in the business system and what condition made it work.
4. **Generate combinations.** Develop options that combine types, such as a partner network with a new profit model and channel, or a service layer with customer-engagement redesign. Write the required capabilities and likely trade-offs for each combination.
5. **Select an innovation mix.** Score options against customer value, strategic fit, economic potential, defensibility, feasibility, and portfolio balance. Choose a few experiments or strategic themes, not ten disconnected initiatives.
## Output Contract
An innovation mix strategy with a current-state type map, evidence-backed gap audit, cross-type option set, selection criteria, prioritized themes, and experiments or design questions. It must show how each recommendation changes the business system beyond a feature and how the choices work together. A feature backlog labeled with ten headings does not meet the contract.
## Evidence
Treat observed offer changes, financial mechanics, customer behavior, and partner arrangements as evidence. A public announcement can indicate intent but not economic success; label it accordingly. State assumptions about customer adoption, channel cooperation, and profit-pool shift. Do not infer that an option is differentiated merely because no direct competitor uses it; the absence may reflect a constraint that the analysis has missed.
## Checks
- The current map covers all ten types, including types absent from the business today.
- Each option names the innovation mechanism and the customer or economic outcome it is meant to change.
- Options combine types only where the dependencies make strategic sense.
- Feature ideas are not allowed to crowd out configuration, process, channel, or experience moves.
- Selected themes identify capabilities, constraints, and falsifiable experiment signals.
## Failure Modes
- Treating the taxonomy as a checklist that requires one initiative in every type.
- Renaming a feature upgrade as “product performance” without changing the offer system.
- Copying a platform's partner network while ignoring the incentives that attract partners.
- Generating novel combinations with no customer problem or economic logic.
- Choosing an option set that demands incompatible channel, pricing, or operating-model changes.
