# SEO Strategy
## Purpose
Design and sequence technical, content, and authority initiatives so the business wins qualified organic demand for high-intent search queries.
## Use
- Organic search is a material acquisition channel and ranking gaps block demand.
- You must prioritize technical, content, and authority work against commercial intent.
- Digital growth plans need a defensible SEO investment and sequencing plan.
## Avoid
- You need paid media ROI allocation across channels—use marketing-mix-modeling.
- The product has no search demand yet and awareness is the issue—use go-to-market-strategy or brand-equity-model.
- You only need a one-off page conversion test—use a-b-testing.
## Inputs
**Must-have:** Current organic performance and ranking baseline; keyword and intent landscape for target segments; technical site health and content inventory; competitive SERP and authority benchmarks.

**Nice-to-have:** Search Console query data, conversion values by landing page, crawl logs, and editorial capacity by subject-matter expert.

**When data is thin:** Start with a small representative query set and manual SERP review. Keep opportunity estimates separate from confirmed search demand.
## Procedure
1. Establish organic goals by qualified outcome: demo requests, store visits, subscriptions, or revenue, not visits alone. Segment the audience, market, device, and language scope.
2. Collect queries and classify each by intent, funnel role, commercial value, current landing page, rank, and SERP feature. Consolidate near-duplicates into topic clusters.
3. Crawl the site to identify indexability, canonicalization, redirects, internal links, page experience defects, and orphan pages. Verify material findings in browser and search-console evidence before assigning work.
4. Map every priority cluster to one preferred URL. Identify gaps requiring a new page, weak pages needing revision, and competing pages that should be merged or redirected.
5. Compare the top results for content depth, format, structured data, link context, and brand evidence. Specify only authority actions the organization can earn legitimately through useful assets, partnerships, or digital PR.
6. Score initiatives by expected qualified demand, intent fit, ranking feasibility, effort, and dependency. Sequence technical blockers before content work that depends on crawl or indexation, then set a measurement cadence.
## Output Contract
Produce an organic-search roadmap containing the prioritized topic-to-URL map, technical remediation backlog, content briefs or refresh list, authority initiatives, owners, dates, dependencies, and success measures. For each item, show the target query cluster, search intent, current baseline, and expected business signal. The roadmap must resolve cannibalization and crawl barriers; it is not a loose keyword export or paid-media budget allocation.
## Evidence
Use platform data for impressions, clicks, queries, and indexed-page status; retain the extraction date because rankings move. Treat search-volume tools as directional estimates and label the market and match type. Do not infer demand from a single competitor’s rank. Separate observed technical defects from hypotheses about algorithmic cause, and never promise a rank or traffic total that the evidence cannot support.
## Checks
- Each priority cluster has one intended canonical landing page.
- Pages proposed for removal have redirect and internal-link plans.
- Content recommendations match the actual intent and SERP format.
- Technical fixes are reproducible with affected URLs and verification steps.
- Success metrics connect organic visibility to a qualified conversion event.
## Failure Modes
- Publishing several articles against the same query and splitting relevance signals.
- Chasing high-volume informational terms that cannot produce the target customer.
- Treating a crawl-tool warning as proof that a page is absent from search.
- Buying low-quality links that add risk without topical relevance.
