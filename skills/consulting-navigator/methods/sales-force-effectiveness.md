# Sales Force Effectiveness
## Purpose
Diagnose and redesign how the sales organization covers accounts, spends time, sells, and is incented to raise productivity and revenue per rep. It connects field behavior to organization design and economics rather than optimizing a single web conversion stage.
## Use
- Sales productivity, coverage, roles, or incentives need a structured diagnostic and redesign
- Growth stalls despite market demand and the sales engine is the suspected bottleneck
- You must improve win rates, call capacity, or revenue per rep with an integrated SFE program
## Avoid
- The problem is purely digital conversion on a website—use sales-funnel-optimization or a-b-testing
- Lead quality is the only issue and sales process is fine—use lead-scoring-methodology first
- You need end-to-end process maps of marketing-to-cash stages only—start with funnel-analysis
## Inputs
**Must-have:** Sales org design, roles, and territory/coverage maps; activity, pipeline, and productivity metrics by rep and segment; comp plan structure and historical attainment; CRM process data and win/loss evidence.

**Nice-to-have:** ride-along notes, manager coaching observations, account potential estimates, sales-cycle time, and competitor intelligence.

**When data is thin:** sample a representative mix of territories and inspect calendar, CRM, and deal records together; do not generalize one high performer's practice to every segment.
## Procedure
1. **Segment the selling system.** Define customer segments, routes to market, roles, and territories. Calculate addressable potential and required coverage by segment before judging rep performance.
2. **Create a rep productivity fact base.** Compare capacity, customer-facing time, opportunities created, conversion, average deal value, quota attainment, and revenue per fully loaded rep. Separate territory opportunity from execution.
3. **Trace time and process friction.** Review a sample of weeks and deals to quantify selling, administration, travel, manager coaching, approval delay, and CRM rework. Observe where handoffs or tools consume selling time.
4. **Test design levers.** Diagnose whether the limiting cause is role specialization, territory balance, account allocation, skill, manager span, sales process, incentive measure, or enablement. Model trade-offs; a redesign that raises calls but lowers account continuity may not win.
5. **Design the productivity roadmap.** Specify coverage changes, role charters, quota and incentive changes, manager routines, capability interventions, pilot populations, and leading metrics. Sequence changes so compensation rules do not contradict the new behavior.
## Output Contract
Deliver a sales operating-model recommendation with segment coverage logic, productivity baseline, root-cause diagnosis, quantified design choices, and a 90-day implementation roadmap. It must state which roles own which customers, how performance will be measured, and how incentives reinforce the desired motion.
## Evidence
CRM activity is observed only after checking completeness; missing calls or stale stages are data-quality findings, not proof of inactivity. Treat territory potential, time allocation from interviews, and anticipated lift as estimates with ranges. Preserve the denominator for every productivity measure: revenue per rep, per selling hour, and per account have different meanings. Disaggregate results by segment and tenure before making role changes.
## Checks
- Territory potential and account assignments are reconciled to the coverage map.
- Productivity comparisons adjust for portfolio opportunity and ramp stage.
- Compensation scenarios are tested against historical attainment and cost.
- Recommendations identify a behavior, owner, metric, and review cadence.
- Pilots include a comparable holdout or before-and-after baseline.
## Failure Modes
- Calling low revenue a skill problem when territories contain very different potential.
- Raising quotas without changing overloaded account books or administrative burden.
- Paying on bookings while asking reps to protect renewal quality.
- Centralizing specialists and accidentally lengthening response time for priority accounts.
- Treating CRM timestamps as reliable despite widespread late entry.
