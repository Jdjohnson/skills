# CFO Decision Rubric

Use this thinking aid for material decisions. It is not a required report.

## Materiality

A decision is material when it could meaningfully change cash, revenue, margin, expense, capacity, recurring-revenue exposure, a personnel or ownership commitment, transaction economics, or the recommended risk posture. Demote cleanup that cannot change a decision.

## Evidence states

Use whichever labels preserve decision-relevant distinctions:

| State | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Confirmed | Observed in an authoritative current source. |
| Signed | Formally committed; delivery, billing, or collection may still be pending. |
| Verbal | A specific party stated intent without a binding agreement. |
| Estimated | Derived from a disclosed method, range, or assumption. |
| Assumed | Needed for a scenario but not evidenced. |
| Missing | Required for confidence and unavailable after reasonable investigation. |

Never treat verbal pipeline as signed backlog, an accounting balance as unrestricted cash, revenue as profit, or available hours as profitable capacity.

## Practicality

Before recommending a move, ask whether it:

1. changes dollars, exposure, or a near-term decision enough to matter;
2. can be executed with available people and systems;
3. is proportionate to the organization's scale; and
4. solves the problem more directly than a recurring administrative process.

If not, move it to **Ignore for now**.

## Relevant financial checks

- **Cash:** Reconcile bank cash, receivables, payables, payroll, taxes, debt, restricted amounts, deferred-delivery costs, and one-time items. Do not double-count obligations or state precise runway when material inputs are missing.
- **Margin:** Separate revenue, external cost, remaining internal labor, contingency, and unpriced scope.
- **Capacity and hiring:** Distinguish historical utilization, forward allocation, role bottlenecks, loaded cost, attributable contribution, sold work, opportunity cost, and reversibility.
- **Recurring revenue:** Test renewal timing, gross margin, concentration, delivery burden, and fixed-cost support.
- **Pipeline:** Keep signed, verbal, probable, and speculative work separate. Probability-weight only with an explicit method.
- **Transactions:** Separate enterprise value, debt, working capital, cash, taxes, fees, owner allocations, and closing adjustments. Check for missing schedules and double counting.

## Independent challenge

Give an authorized reviewer only what is needed to test the call:

- the proposed conclusion;
- decisive evidence and evidence states;
- supporting math or ranges;
- the strongest counterargument already considered; and
- a request to find factual errors, double counting, unsupported certainty, missing alternatives, or a simpler higher-value move.

Resolve each material challenge as accepted, rejected with reason, or superseded by better evidence. Independent review informs the call; it does not approve it or require consensus.
