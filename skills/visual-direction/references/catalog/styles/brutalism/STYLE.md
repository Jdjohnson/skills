# Brutalism

## Defining grammar

Brutalism makes construction legible. Mass, joints, service runs, and the imprint of formwork become the visual argument instead of receiving a decorative skin. The character is civic, weighty, and materially direct. Use it when a message needs public seriousness, structural honesty, or deliberate resistance to polished commercial softness. Roughness is evidence of making, not random distress.

## Composition

Build with large interlocking volumes, deep recesses, hard crops, and a grid that feels derived from bays or load paths. A single cantilevered block can dominate several quieter fields. Negative space should register as a carved void. Keep perspective frontal or axially dramatic; fussy diagonals and scattered fragments weaken the sense that every element carries weight.

## Type

Choose a compact grotesk, neo-grotesk, or plain engineered sans in decisive weights. Headlines may become slabs within the architecture, but their hierarchy must remain obvious. Use few sizes, short lines, and blunt alignment changes. Small information can sit in disciplined columns. Avoid genteel serifs, decorative ligatures, and fashionable microtype that turns severity into boutique styling.

## Color and material

Center concrete gray, soot black, chalk, stone, and restrained oxidized metal. Color may appear as one construction or safety accent, never as a cosmetic rainbow. Surface detail should reveal aggregate, board marks, seams, weathering, and honest shadow. Do not confuse material truth with a generic grunge overlay; texture follows the object and its method of assembly.

## Imagery and light

Favor monumental structures, close studies of poured surfaces, repetitive structural bays, and human figures used only to establish scale. Low raking light exposes relief; overcast light clarifies mass without glamour. Crop so beams and slabs continue beyond the frame. When generating an approximation, specify recognizable movement-level concrete massing, exposed construction logic, and severe civic scale.

## Common exits

The style exits when raw concrete becomes a luxury backdrop, when arbitrary scratches replace structural detail, or when every object is merely gray. Industrial warehouse imagery is lighter, more repetitive, and operational; scaffold structure is temporary and skeletal. Web brutalism shares bluntness but works through interface defaults and information exposure. Preserve finished architectural conviction rather than ruin, rubble, or dystopian spectacle.

## Medium adaptation

In identity systems, translate mass into oversized blocks, cropped typography, and a narrow material palette. On websites, retain a strict grid and high contrast while protecting legibility and keyboard behavior. In presentations, let one structural image or claim carry each spread. Packaging can use uncoated stock, blind embossing, and heavy geometry without pretending paper is concrete. Motion should reveal planes through measured shifts, long shadows, or slow sectional assembly rather than energetic collage.
