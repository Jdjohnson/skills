# Sticker Sheet System

## Core grammar

Sticker Sheet System is a manufactured family of peelable motifs, not a mood board of icons. Visible backing, kiss-cut contours, consistent offsets, collectible variation, repeat scale, and efficient nesting make the object legible as a sheet. Use it for merchandise, reward systems, planners, packaging inserts, digital sticker packs, and visual identities built from modular collectibles.

## Composition and hierarchy

Arrange a clear sheet boundary first. Mix a few large anchor motifs with smaller fillers while preserving enough backing around every cut path. Rotation and scale changes can improve nesting, but the family should still feel intentional. One partially lifted corner or peeled gap can explain use. Avoid a rigid corporate logo grid unless the subject truly is a label system.

## Type and graphic language

Short labels, micro-phrases, and tiny badges may join the motif family if their outlines and color behavior match. Use one sturdy face with generous internal counters. Text cannot become so small that the physical cut shape carries only illegible noise. Do not present client marks or familiar platform emoji as generic stickers.

## Color, material, imagery, and light

Flat illustrated color, bold contour, and a consistent white or clear offset are typical. Matte paper, glossy vinyl, holographic film, and translucent stock change both edge and backing behavior. Lighting should reveal the sheet surface and cut lines without turning the composition into a deep product scene. A restrained shadow under one lifted sticker can prove peelability.

## Use across media

For print, document bleed, cut contour, safe interior, registration, and material. Packaging should show the full sheet at a useful scale. Digital packs can borrow the family and offset logic even without physical backing, but should preserve coordinated variation. Motion can animate peel, lift, and placement rather than bouncing every icon at once.

## Common exits

A single contour-cut graphic is a sticker, not a system. A brand presentation with logos in boxes does not demonstrate manufacturing. Chaotic layered decals move toward Sticker Bomb. Squash, stretch, and speed marks belong to Motion Sticker. Avoid spacing so tight that adjacent cut paths collide.

## Direction recipe

Create a family of ten motifs with shared line, palette, and subject logic. Draw consistent offsets, nest them inside one visible sheet, and reserve margins for handling. Add one peel cue, print a scale proof, and inspect the smallest interior details before approving the composition.
