# Intake

Draft the brief for the user instead of making them design the research process.

Settle only decisions that materially affect:

- the useful outcome;
- scope and exclusions;
- evidence expectations;
- private inputs and who may receive them;
- the first valuable research questions.

Ask one compact batch of questions. Recommend defaults wherever possible. Do not ask about coordinator tasks, runners, models, heartbeats, review protocols, or other implementation mechanics.

Write `brief.md` with:

- title;
- useful outcome;
- boundaries and exclusions;
- evidence expectations;
- approved private inputs;
- starting questions.

Research remains source-backed and research-only. The current task remains the visible coordinator. Those are fixed operating rules, not intake choices.
