# Week Review

1. Compare planned outcomes with evidence from the week. Mark each done, partial, moved, or missed without inventing external state.
2. Surface concrete movement, changed deadlines, and observable patterns in energy, work type, or calendar load.
3. Bring stale carryover to a decision: keep live, move to a named future period, wait on a dependency, or drop.
4. Ask a few specific reflection questions, one at a time, about how the week felt, what mattered, and what should change.
5. Propose the recap and wait for approval. Update only the configured recap area, preserving the original plan.

List requested external changes separately for exact approval.
