# Day Close

Lead with evidence rather than a blank questionnaire.

1. Present what is proven done, what moved or did not happen, exact unresolved lifecycle states, and unclear local statuses.
2. Ask one useful question at a time about what landed, what did not, current energy, and tomorrow's re-entry.
3. Propose a compact capture: done, moved/missed with known reason, alignment, energy in the user's words, and one tomorrow line.
4. Ask for changes before writing. After approval, update the existing closeout area and affected local work rows without rewriting the morning plan.
5. List optional external task, time, meeting, or delegation follow-ups separately. Do not perform them automatically.

Do not mark unfinished work moved or assign a new date unless the user actually makes that decision.
