# Week Plan

Co-create the week in phases, pausing for revision:

1. shape and carryover;
2. constraints and realistic capacity;
3. three concrete outcomes and one central theme;
4. deduplicated tasks, meetings, deadlines, and preparation;
5. day-by-day rhythm, buffer, and outcome ownership;
6. concrete movement in named work and relationship lanes;
7. explicit items that are waiting or not this week.

After approval, update the configured week record while preserving its prior recap and completed material. Suggest a morning mode afterward only when today still needs a plan.
