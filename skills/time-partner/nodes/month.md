# Month Plan

Co-create the month in phases:

1. prior patterns, carryover, and calendar shape;
2. constraints and capacity;
3. three meaningful outcomes and a central theme;
4. period-relevant tasks, deadlines, and decisions;
5. a useful week-by-week rhythm;
6. specific work, relationship, health, or personal intentions only when the user states or approves them;
7. explicit items that are waiting or not this month.

After approval, update the configured month record while preserving completed material and recap history.
