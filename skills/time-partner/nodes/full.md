# Full Morning

Move one phase at a time and wait after each prompt.

1. Give a short read on the day's carryover, calendar shape, pressure, and useful open space.
2. Invite an open journal, then ask separately about energy and sleep. Ask configured recurring personal prompts one at a time.
3. Present known constraints and ask for corrections.
4. Propose three grounded priorities and one central thing; explain each briefly and invite revision.
5. Show one editable work table containing only real tasks, meetings, deadlines, and decisions. Dedupe and keep local statuses stable.
6. Review only legitimate parking-lot candidates.
7. After alignment, preserve the raw entry and update the configured day record without erasing existing work.

The durable record should keep the raw journal first, followed by a concise day narrative, priorities, constraints, central focus, editable work table, parking lot, log, and closeout area unless the workspace template defines another order.
